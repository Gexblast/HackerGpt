Gann Terminal automates your manual workflow: the 09:15–09:30 opening-range swing high/low becomes the box; ratio levels 0 / 0.26 / 0.382 / 0.5 / 0.618 / 0.7 / 1 (plus ±ext) are projected from it. Every level is then scored with the full options dataset — OI walls, level confluence (OI/gamma/IV/vol/history), gamma-flip zone, dealer pressure, order flow — to answer "which level will it turn from?": each gets a strength /100 and, when price reaches it, a BOUNCE vs BREAK verdict. Entry = a fresh RSI(14)xSMA(9) cross AT the level agreeing with the verdict. Alerts: 📐 AT LEVEL (soft) and ✅ GANN ENTRY (strong). Signals are probabilistic and NOT backtested — paper-trade and log outcomes before risking real capital.
